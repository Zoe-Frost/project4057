ABOUT

Our story

The team of 4057 have always valued a more sustainable future where we are not trying to stop the progression of technology but rather ensure that we are taking care of the waste said progression is leaving behind.

In some corner or hidden draw in the house, we all have a secret stash of old wires, phones, and technology that can no longer be used, but we hold onto it because we have all been told certain parts cannot be recycled.

This lead us to explore how we could raise awareness into this issue in a manner than makes you want to keep scrolling, all the way through to the solutions that we found in local areas in NZ, starting with Tauranga!

The mission

Connecting users to local solutions

Our mission is to connect Gen Z kiwis living in Tauranga to local solutions while giving them an experience of what their home could one day become if we don’t do something about our e-waste habits now.

Currently, New Zealand is generating around 80,000+ tonnes of e-waste every year, and yet majority of us have nowhere to place it, leaving it in hidden draws and boxes around our homes.

Therefore, we aim to connect you to local solutions in Tauranga where you can take your e-waste and dispose of it properly.

Top 5 FAQ’s

Why does e-waste matter?

E-waste is currently the largest growing threat to our environment, and due to the fact the materials used in creating technology cannot be broken down by the environment for a very long time if at all, they sit there and as that grows so does the threat to our environment.

Will there be other versions of this around NZ?

Yes! 4057 will be spreading around New Zealand, starting with Tauranga which is where the project originated from.

Isn’t e-waste not able to be recycled?

E-waste can not be recycled in the typical way, however, there are companies and businesses who will take e-waste and break it down or send it onto bigger companies overseas who do have the tools to contain it and deal with it properly.

Where can I find my local solutions?

Visit our map found at the top of the screen on the menu and you will be able to see lit up dots on a map of Tauranga, click on them to see the business and general information about them.
